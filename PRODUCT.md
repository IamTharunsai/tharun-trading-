# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Confirmed: the owner is building a personal autonomous trading platform and needs to understand, supervise and improve every decision, position and supporting service.

## Product Purpose

Provide one coherent place to inspect markets, evaluate opportunities, preserve capital, execute through supported brokers, reconcile actual fills and learn from attributable outcomes. The owner wants server-side autonomy that does not depend on opening manual research chats.

## Operating Context

Existing React/TypeScript frontend on Vercel, Node/Express/TypeScript backend and Python forecasting service on Railway, with PostgreSQL. The current reviewed production revision is a7e27b3. Paper/live execution is distinct from website deployment. Source review has found material defects, simulated research output and incomplete integration; no profitability claim is established.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Requested scope includes stocks, crypto, prediction markets, forecasts, council decisions, risk limits, trades, portfolio analytics, journal, research, durable memory, provider health and full feature testing. Voice, gesture, 3D interaction and phone unlocking are requested additions, not current verified capabilities. Every financial display needs data provenance and a freshness state. Broker order/fill state must match what the UI reports.

## Brand Commitments

Confirmed name: **Bhishma**, as explicitly selected by the owner. The owner wants a distinctive experience inspired by strategic judgment and the Mahabharata character, including ambitious motion and spatial interaction. Visible product branding must avoid Apex and artificial-intelligence marketing terms. No claim of guaranteed profit or perfect forecasting is supported.

## Evidence on Hand

The baseline source, hashed file/route/control/environment inventories, safe Railway configuration summary, source findings and isolated critical test results are retained in the sibling bhishma-review directory. Some existing screens use random financial output; those are defects to replace, not design proof. No verified historical net-of-cost strategy qualification currently exists.

## Product Principles

- Capital protection has deterministic authority over research recommendations.
- Every decision is attributable to timestamped evidence, a real quorum and an executable venue.
- Unknown, stale, simulated and verified results have distinct visible states.
- Automation includes reliable exits, reconciliation, restart recovery and honest fault reporting.
- Strategy allocation must earn renewal through net-of-cost evidence; essential safety services remain active.

## Open Decisions

The owner has now explicitly chosen a systematic workspace grouped around stocks, crypto, options/futures, prediction markets and the current account. The home workspace must prioritize actual open positions, synchronize research before and after placement, and connect each decision to its evidence, calculations and learning outcome. Manual research triggers must have a clear role alongside server-side monitoring. Visual implementation and voice/gesture permissions still require device validation. MiroFish and Graphify integration must be evaluated and implemented with appropriate boundaries; neither is currently connected by this branch.
