# Unscheduled automatic holding maintenance

Base `755378cffd25e2dee2429768a6f5c9bac250e0fc`. This is a complete internal runner, not activation: no route, cron registration, mode setting or qualification producer is added. Default mode is OBSERVE; REDUCE must be explicitly selected by an internal caller. Existing production scans remain paper-only.

The runner selects the configured owner's actual US opening executions, recovers existing known order IDs before REDUCE planning, verifies current broker inventory against linked remaining ownership, loads a fresh persisted forecast for that same venue/market, and applies the existing edge-flip/evidence-change/convergence rules. Unknown commission, uncertain submissions, external inventory differences, missing/stale forecasts, closed markets and nonfinite/stale books do not become reduction readiness. Entry kill, paper entry mode and expired opening qualification do not prevent a proven reduction of a real live holding.

Held markets use the documented [public get-by-slug endpoint](https://docs.polymarket.us/api-reference/markets/get-market-by-slug) with bounded responses, redirects disabled and shared read admission. A holding outside the first 500 active listing results is still inspectable; repeated full-universe listing cannot redate policy evidence or consume its entire freshness budget.

Only executable depth at the observed book is considered. Contract quantity is rounded DOWN to market precision and capped by both owned and observed quantity, with no comparison tolerance authorizing excess sale. The gross minimum sale price is the worst executable economic-side price rounded down to the YES price grid (SELL_SHORT complements it to a YES limit). Convergence uses a conservative commission upper bound from the verified coefficient; those estimates never book fees or P&L. Actual cumulative order evidence retains accounting authority.

Each decision has a stable opening/forecast/remaining-quantity key; repeated evaluation or restart cannot repeat a zero-fill decision. Partial fills change remaining ownership and can produce a separately evidenced remainder decision. Policy evidence is stored on the durable reduction. The final writer re-reads the forecast and opening baseline, verifies their identities and fingerprints, requires the planned forecast still be the latest, and retains the original quote/forecast expiry. Later market reads cannot redate stale policy evidence.

OBSERVE creates no holding, claim, order or qualification and does not reconcile state. Its ready status describes a candidate reduction only. REDUCE performs existing read recovery, then executes eligible decisions through the same guarded reduction writer. Complete settlement, account-wide historical loss coverage and financial entry qualification remain uncertified; this runner cannot authorize new entry or close a ledger from public resolution or a missing position.

Batches exclude holdings closed by linked orders and visit at most 20 opening identities in order. REDUCE retains a separate cursor on existing owner recovery state; OBSERVE keeps read-only traversal progress in memory. Missing/stale evidence on earlier identities cannot permanently starve later holdings.

The official [orders overview](https://docs.polymarket.us/api-reference/orders/overview#understanding-price-with-order-intent) defines SELL_LONG/SELL_SHORT and YES-denominated price polarity. The existing strict [get-order](https://docs.polymarket.us/api-reference/orders/get-order) cumulative parser owns actual fills and commissions. No private provider/account request is made during development. Acceptance requires network-disabled CI: actual opening entry → policy → partial sale → known-ID recovery → remaining basis, plus restart/zero-fill, quote/forecast mutation, NO polarity, fee uncertainty and manual inventory discrepancy fixtures.
