# Bhishma: strategic command experience

Owner clarification, 7 October 2026: the ambition is exceptional overall experience and strategic judgment, beyond a conventional Jarvis-style dashboard. This document defines the direction; it does not claim implemented features.

## The central experience

Opening Bhishma should answer three questions immediately: what needs attention, where capital is exposed, and which decisions have earned action. A quiet command surface leads into a spatial strategy map. The map connects positions, catalysts, evidence, dissent and exit conditions, with a complete accessible table alternative.

Mahabharata inspiration comes through disciplined composition, restrained ceremonial motion, architectural depth and an oath to preserve capital. Warm ivory, charcoal and aged bronze establish the identity; semantic colors retain their financial meaning. Original art and deliberate typography should carry the character rather than generic glowing panels.

## Four connected spaces

1. **Command:** exposure, available capital, urgent faults, broker reconciliation and the next required action. Paper or live authority is always visible.
2. **Strategy:** opportunity map, competing scenarios, evidence freshness, contradictory observations and conditional plans. Research that lacks evidence stays visibly unavailable.
3. **Council:** inspect each specialist's source, opening case, challenge, final reason and eligibility. Follow one decision from observation to approved allocation and actual fill.
4. **Chronicle:** outcome attribution, costs, mistakes, forecast calibration and strategy renewal. Memory supports inspection of how a lesson changed a later decision.

## Interaction standards

- Progressive depth: summary first, supporting evidence on demand, source and calculation available without leaving the decision.
- Spatial motion communicates relationships and state changes. Reduced-motion and low-power modes provide the same information.
- Voice supports navigation, questions and preparation of actions. Financial actions require visible review, authenticated authority and explicit confirmation.
- Gesture interaction is optional. Every action also works with keyboard, pointer and touch.
- Phone unlocking uses an established authentication mechanism such as passkeys; decorative recognition must never substitute for authorization.
- Sound and animation are subordinate to readability, response time and operational accuracy.
- A trade has distinct prepared, submitted, partially filled, filled, rejected and reconciled states. Animation cannot imply a fill before broker evidence arrives.

## Proof required before acceptance

Review every existing screen and control against a coverage ledger. Validate navigation, keyboard operation, small screens, reduced motion, loading, empty, stale, disconnected and error states. Measure performance on representative devices. Test the research-to-order journey and exit recovery separately from visual quality. Evaluate financial benefit using attributable net results and out-of-sample evidence; presentation cannot establish profitability.
